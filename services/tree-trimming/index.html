BEFORE YOU CALL

Common questions.

What is the difference between trimming and pruning?+

People often use the terms interchangeably. Trimming commonly describes managing growth or clearance, while pruning may focus on selected branches. Explain the outcome you want rather than worrying about the terminology.

When should I have a tree trimmed?+

Timing depends on the species, condition, and reason for the work. Describe your concerns when you call; an on-site assessment may be needed before deciding on timing.

Can trimming help with branches over my roof?+

It may be an option, depending on the tree and the amount of clearance needed. Share the location of the branches and any nearby wires when discussing the work.
