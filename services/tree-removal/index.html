The tree and its surroundings

Tree size, condition, nearby buildings, fences, and overhead utilities all affect how a removal can be approached. Describe these when you call.

Access to the work area

Driveways, gates, slopes, and the distance from the road can affect equipment access. Mention narrow spaces or soft ground before an on-site visit.

BEFORE YOU CALL

Common questions.

How much does tree removal cost?+

There is no useful one-price-fits-all answer. Size, condition, access, nearby obstacles, and the amount of hauling all affect the scope. Call to discuss an assessment of your property.

Does removal include the stump?+

Ask specifically about stump removal or grinding and whether it is offered. Tree removal and stump work are different tasks, and any stump work should be clearly listed in the estimate.

Can a tree near utility wires be removed?+

Tell the company about overhead wires before arranging work. Do not approach or touch a tree or branch contacting a power line; contact your utility and emergency services when appropriate.
