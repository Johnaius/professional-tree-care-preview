WHEN TO CALL

Common jobs.

  • Fallen branches and limbs after rough weather
  • Brush piles left over from property projects
  • Cut wood and woody debris taking up yard space

Confirm service availability and scope directly.

01

Identify the material

Describe the mix of branches, logs, brush, and any non-wood material. Photos and an approximate pile size can help explain the job when you speak with the company.

02

Describe where it is

Material beside a driveway is different from debris behind a fence or down a slope. Access and hauling distance matter when discussing cleanup.

03

Agree on the finish

Clarify what will be removed, what can be left as firewood or chips, and what the final cleanup includes. Do not assume soil repair or landscaping is part of debris removal.

BEFORE YOU CALL

Common questions.

Can I ask about cleanup without removing a standing tree?+

Yes. Describe the branches, brush, or logs you already have and ask about availability for a cleanup-only job.

Can you help with cleanup after a storm?+

Call about fallen branches and woody debris after a storm in Buffalo or Western New York. Scheduling depends on the work and availability; no immediate or guaranteed emergency response is promised. Stay away from unstable trees and downed wires, and contact emergency services or your utility for immediate hazards.

Will the wood be hauled away?+

That needs to be agreed as part of the job. Ask about hauling, chipping, and leaving wood on site, and make sure your preference is reflected in the estimate.
